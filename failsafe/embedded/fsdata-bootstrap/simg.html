Write SIMG

The image is streamed to the flash in erase-block sized chunks, starting at offset 0x0.

WARNINGS
  • writing a wrong image will overwrite the whole flash, including bootloaders
  • do not power off the device while the image is being written
  • a full dump may take several minutes, do not close the browser
  • bad blocks are skipped, a dump taken from another unit may not boot
